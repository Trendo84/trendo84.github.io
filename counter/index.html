Straight answers

Where does the price data come from?

Publicly listed prices from each merchant's own site, collected daily, plus licensed product feeds where a merchant offers one. Counter shows what each retailer publishes — it never guesses, never interpolates, and marks a line as unavailable rather than showing you a stale number.

Are these my trade prices?

No. They're published list prices. Trade pricing is negotiated per account and no outside party can see yours. Set your discount per merchant in settings and every comparison recalculates against what you actually pay.

Is Counter affiliated with any of these retailers?

No. No partnership, no endorsement, no sponsorship. Counter is independent, and it stays independent — that's the entire value of it. Where a merchant runs an affiliate program we may earn a commission on a click-through, and any page where that applies says so.

Do you sell my search data?

No. Your searches, your watch list and your discount settings are yours. They aren't sold, and they aren't shared with the merchants you're comparing.

My merchant isn't listed. Can you add it?

Ask. The launch list is the five biggest by trade volume; after that the order is set by what people request. Regional and specialist suppliers are wanted — those are usually where the widest spreads hide.

When does it launch?

Early access opens first, at the launch price locked for the first year. Put your email in and you'll get one message when it's live — no drip sequence, no newsletter.
